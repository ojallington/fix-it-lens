# Wat vond je ervan? / What did you think?

Fix-It Lens · feedback after a session · form version 1 (27 Oct 2026)

Asked of pilot households only: in the app after a pilot session, or as a fillable PDF. About two minutes; every
question may be skipped. The answers are kept in `FIXIT_PRIVATE/feedback/`, never in the repository. The summary
(`python -m eval.feedback summary`) prints totals and, word for word, only the answers whose writer ticked a quote box.
The key in each `<!-- -->` names the answer in the record; it is not shown.
