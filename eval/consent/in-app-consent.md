# Toestemming in de app / In-app consent

Fix-It Lens · in-app consent · version 1 (27 Oct 2026)

Shown once, before the first appliance is added, to anyone who comes in through the open door or the judges' link
and signs no pilot form. The box must be ticked to go on. Each half fits one phone screen (under 90 words). The plate
sentence says "blacked out" only once the serial is blacked out on every stored plate photo; until then it says
"kept privately".

---

## Nederlands

**Voordat je begint**

Terwijl je een taak doet, stuurt de app camerabeelden en korte clips naar de AI-dienst van Meta, die je apparaat
bekijkt en elke stap controleert. Meta traint zijn AI er niet mee, maar mag ze bewaren om op misbruik te controleren.

Wij bewaren de apparaten van je huishouden, je taken en de uitkomst van elke stap. Foto's van typeplaatjes bewaren
wij privé.

Je kunt alles altijd laten wissen: in de app of via pilot@craftycast.com.

☐ Ik ben 18 jaar of ouder en ga hiermee akkoord.

---

## English

**Before you start**

While you do a job, the app sends camera pictures and short clips to Meta's AI service, to see your appliance and
check each step. Meta does not train its AI on them, but may keep them to check for misuse.

We store your household's appliances, jobs and the result of each step. Photos of type plates are kept privately.

You can delete it all at any time in the app, or by emailing pilot@craftycast.com.

☐ I am 18 or older and agree to this.
